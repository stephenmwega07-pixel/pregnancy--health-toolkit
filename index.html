My Medication Schedule

Pregnancy Guidelines

First Trimester (Weeks 1–12)

  • Book your first antenatal appointment.
  • Discuss recommended supplements with your clinician.
  • Avoid alcohol and smoking.
  • Seek professional advice before taking medicine.

Second Trimester (Weeks 13–27)

  • Attend scheduled antenatal visits.
  • Eat a balanced diet.
  • Discuss pregnancy screening with your provider.
  • Ask about safe physical activities.

Third Trimester (Weeks 28–Birth)

  • Attend regular antenatal appointments.
  • Discuss your birth plan.
  • Monitor your baby's movements.
  • Prepare for delivery.

Pregnancy Nutrition Guide

  • Eat vegetables and fruits.
  • Include protein-rich foods.
  • Eat foods rich in iron.
  • Drink safe, clean water.
  • Choose well-cooked foods.
  • Avoid unpasteurized dairy products.

Antenatal Care Information

Antenatal care helps monitor the health of both mother and baby during pregnancy.

  • Blood pressure checks.
  • Blood and urine tests when indicated.
  • Baby growth monitoring.
  • Pregnancy health education.
  • Birth preparation.

Pregnancy Danger Signs

Seek urgent medical attention if you experience:

  • Heavy vaginal bleeding.
  • Severe abdominal pain.
  • Severe headache or blurred vision.
  • Difficulty breathing.
  • Convulsions or fainting.
  • Fluid leaking from the vagina.
  • Reduced or absent baby movements.

Do not wait for a website reminder when experiencing these symptoms.
